10 Short Questions with Answers

Each question is followed by a clear answer for revision and exam preparation.

  1. What is a learning theory?

    Answer: A learning theory explains how people gain knowledge, develop skills, remember information, and build understanding.

  2. What is behaviourism?

    Answer: Behaviourism explains learning through practice, observable behaviour, reinforcement, and feedback.

  3. How can digital tools support behaviourism?

    Answer: Digital quizzes, flashcards, and drills provide repeated practice and immediate feedback that can strengthen correct responses.

  4. What is cognitivism?

    Answer: Cognitivism focuses on mental processes such as attention, memory, understanding, and information processing.

  5. How can a teacher support learning according to cognitivism?

    Answer: A teacher can organise information into smaller parts and use clear explanations, concept maps, diagrams, and examples connected with prior knowledge.

  6. What is constructivism?

    Answer: Constructivism views learners as active participants who build knowledge through exploration, problem solving, and experience.

  7. Give one digital example of constructivist learning.

    Answer: Students can use a science simulation, explore different variables, observe results, and explain what they discovered.

  8. What is social learning theory?

    Answer: It explains that people can learn by observing others, modelling behaviour, and interacting with peers or teachers.

  9. What is connectivism?

    Answer: Connectivism explains learning through connections with people, digital information, and online networks.

  10. Why should pedagogy guide technology selection?

    Answer: Pedagogy helps the teacher choose a tool that matches the learning goal, teaching method, and needs of the learners.
