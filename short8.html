10 Short Questions with Answers

Each question is followed by a clear answer for revision and exam preparation.

  1. What is technology integration?

    Answer: Technology integration is the planned use of suitable tools and resources to support curriculum, teaching methods, assessment, and learner needs.

  2. What does TPACK stand for?

    Answer: TPACK stands for Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge.

  3. Explain the three main parts of TPACK.

    Answer: Content knowledge is knowledge of the subject. Pedagogical knowledge is knowledge of teaching methods. Technological knowledge is knowledge of tools and their use.

  4. What is the SAMR model?

    Answer: SAMR is a framework that describes four levels of technology use: Substitution, Augmentation, Modification, and Redefinition.

  5. What is digital citizenship?

    Answer: Digital citizenship means using technology safely, respectfully, responsibly, and thoughtfully.

  6. What is a digital footprint?

    Answer: A digital footprint is the information and activity a person leaves online, such as posts, comments, and shared content.

  7. Why should teachers protect student privacy?

    Answer: Student information such as names, grades, and contact details is personal and should only be collected, stored, and shared appropriately.

  8. What is copyright?

    Answer: Copyright is legal protection given to original work such as books, images, videos, music, and presentations.

  9. What is the digital divide?

    Answer: The digital divide is the gap between people who have access to devices, internet, and digital skills and those who do not.

  10. How can teachers make digital learning more inclusive?

    Answer: Teachers can use captions, readable materials, alternative text, flexible ways to participate, and offline options where possible.
