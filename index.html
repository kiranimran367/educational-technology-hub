University Course Lesson Plan

Technology in Education

An eight week learning plan for BS 7th Semester, exploring educational technology, learning theories, instructional design, digital resources, online learning, assessment, emerging technologies, and responsible integration.

BS 7th SemesterDuration: 8 WeeksCourse: Technology in Education

Prepared by: Aleesha Irfan (2023-1717) and Kiran Imran (2023-1718)

Course Overview

This course introduces students to the concepts, tools, methods, and responsible practices involved in using technology for teaching and learning. Each week includes learning objectives, key topics, study material, a learning activity, a related video resource, and separate question practice.

Learning Outcomes

  • Explain key concepts and theories related to Technology in Education.
  • Plan technology supported learning experiences using instructional design principles.
  • Select and evaluate digital resources, LMS platforms, and assessment tools.
  • Discuss the opportunities and limitations of emerging educational technologies.
  • Apply ethical, accessible, and inclusive practices when integrating technology.
