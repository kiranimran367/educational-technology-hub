Week Overview

A Learning Management System (LMS) is a digital platform used to organise courses, share resources, communicate with learners, collect assignments, and monitor progress. Online learning may be synchronous, asynchronous, or blended.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:

  • Define an LMS and identify its common functions.
  • Differentiate synchronous and asynchronous learning.
  • Explain blended learning and online course organisation.
  • Identify good practices for online participation and learner support.

Topics and Study Notes

LMS meaning and functions

An LMS may provide course pages, announcements, learning materials, assignment submission, quizzes, grades, discussion forums, and progress reports.

Synchronous learning

Teacher and learners meet at the same time using live classes, video meetings, or real time discussions.

Asynchronous learning

Learners use recorded lessons, readings, discussion boards, and tasks at different times.

Blended learning

Blended learning combines face to face teaching with planned online learning activities.

Online learning support

Clear instructions, regular feedback, accessible resources, respectful communication, and a predictable course structure help learners participate.

Related Study Material

  1. Explore the features of an LMS such as Moodle or Google Classroom.
  2. Compare synchronous and asynchronous learning with examples.
  3. Draft a simple online course layout with weekly topics and activities.

Suggested video resource: Watch related educational videos on YouTube

Class Activity

Design a sample LMS course page for one week. Include an announcement, reading, video, assignment, and discussion prompt.
