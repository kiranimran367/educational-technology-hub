10 Short Questions with Answers

Each question is followed by a clear answer for revision and exam preparation.

  1. What is instructional design?

    Answer: It is a systematic process of planning lessons, courses, activities, resources, and assessments to support effective learning.

  2. What does ADDIE stand for?

    Answer: ADDIE stands for Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, and Evaluation.

  3. What happens in the Analysis stage?

    Answer: The teacher identifies learners' needs, prior knowledge, learning context, available resources, and possible barriers.

  4. What is the purpose of the Design stage?

    Answer: The teacher sets learning objectives, organises content, selects teaching strategies, plans activities, and decides how learning will be assessed.

  5. What is done during Development?

    Answer: The teacher creates or collects materials such as slides, videos, worksheets, quizzes, and digital activities.

  6. Explain the Implementation stage.

    Answer: The planned lesson is delivered. The teacher guides learners, shares resources, manages activities, and provides support.

  7. What is Evaluation in ADDIE?

    Answer: Evaluation checks whether the lesson and materials were effective and whether learners achieved the objectives.

  8. Differentiate formative and summative evaluation.

    Answer: Formative evaluation takes place during learning to improve instruction. Summative evaluation takes place at the end to judge achievement or overall effectiveness.

  9. Why should learning objectives be measurable?

    Answer: Measurable objectives clearly state what learners should be able to do and help teachers plan suitable activities and assessments.

  10. Give one example of a measurable learning objective.

    Answer: By the end of the lesson, students will be able to list and explain the five stages of the ADDIE model.
