10 Short Questions with Answers

Each question is followed by a clear answer for revision and exam preparation.

  1. What is Technology in Education?

    Answer: It is the planned use of tools, resources, methods, and systems to improve teaching, learning, communication, and educational management.

  2. Why is educational technology more than using gadgets?

    Answer: It includes people, teaching methods, planning, learning resources, and assessment. A gadget is useful only when it supports a learning purpose.

  3. What is the difference between traditional and digital media?

    Answer: Traditional media include books, charts, chalkboards, radio, and television. Digital media include computers, tablets, apps, websites, and online platforms.

  4. Name four examples of educational technology.

    Answer: Examples include printed textbooks, projectors, computers, educational apps, online learning platforms, and interactive whiteboards.

  5. Briefly describe the development of educational technology.

    Answer: It developed from oral teaching and printed books to radio, television, computers, the internet, mobile learning, and AI supported tools.

  6. Write two benefits of educational technology.

    Answer: It can provide wider access to information and support interactive learning. It can also give quick feedback and allow flexible study.

  7. Mention two challenges of using technology in education.

    Answer: Common challenges include poor internet access, high costs, lack of training, distraction, and privacy risks.

  8. How can technology support different learning needs?

    Answer: It can provide captions, audio, adjustable text, visual explanations, practice activities, and flexible ways to access learning materials.

  9. What is meant by purposeful use of technology?

    Answer: It means selecting and using a tool because it supports a clear learning objective, not simply because the tool is new or attractive.

  10. What should a teacher consider before selecting a teaching tool?

    Answer: The teacher should consider the learning objective, learners' needs, topic, available resources, accessibility, cost, and how learning will be assessed.
