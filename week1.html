Week Overview

Technology in Education refers to the purposeful use of digital and non-digital tools, systems, and resources to improve teaching, learning, communication, and educational management. It includes audio visual media, networks, mobile devices, learning platforms, and accessible learning tools.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:

  • Define Technology in Education and explain its scope.
  • Differentiate educational technology from simply using gadgets.
  • Identify major stages in the development of educational technology.
  • Explain benefits and challenges of technology supported learning.

Topics and Study Notes

Meaning and scope

Educational technology combines people, processes, ideas, and tools to solve learning problems. It includes planning, designing, delivering, and evaluating instruction.

Traditional and digital media

Traditional media include chalkboards, printed books, radio, and television. Digital media include computers, tablets, interactive displays, apps, and online platforms.

Development over time

Educational tools developed from oral instruction and printed materials to radio, television, computers, the internet, mobile learning, and AI supported systems.

Benefits and limitations

Technology can widen access, support feedback, and offer different ways to learn. Barriers include cost, connectivity, distraction, privacy risks, and unequal access.

Related Study Material

  1. Read the course notes on definitions, scope, and historical development.
  2. Compare one traditional teaching aid with one digital teaching aid.
  3. Prepare a short example showing how a tool can solve a learning problem.

Suggested video resource: Watch related educational videos on YouTube

Class Activity

In small groups, choose one classroom topic and suggest a suitable teaching tool. Explain why the tool fits the learners, topic, and available resources.
