10 Short Questions with Answers

Each question is followed by a clear answer for revision and exam preparation.

  1. What are digital learning resources?

    Answer: They are electronic materials used to support learning, such as e books, presentations, videos, podcasts, websites, simulations, and digital worksheets.

  2. What is multimedia learning?

    Answer: Multimedia learning uses two or more forms of communication, such as text, pictures, audio, video, or animation, to explain information.

  3. How can images support learning?

    Answer: Images and diagrams can show objects, processes, relationships, and details that may be difficult to explain through words alone.

  4. Why are videos useful in education?

    Answer: Videos can show demonstrations, movement, real life examples, and processes that learners may not be able to observe directly.

  5. What is cognitive load?

    Answer: Cognitive load is the mental effort required to process information. Too much information at once can make learning difficult.

  6. Write three principles of good multimedia design.

    Answer: Use readable text, organise related information together, and remove unnecessary decoration. Clear headings and simple layouts also help.

  7. What is accessibility in digital learning?

    Answer: Accessibility means designing digital materials so learners with different abilities and needs can use them.

  8. Why should videos include captions?

    Answer: Captions make spoken information available as text and support learners who are deaf, hard of hearing, or studying without sound.

  9. How can a teacher check the quality of an online resource?

    Answer: The teacher should check its accuracy, author, organisation, date, relevance, readability, evidence, and suitability for learners.

  10. Why is copyright important when using digital resources?

    Answer: Copyright protects creators' work. Teachers and students should follow permission or licence rules and give credit to sources.
