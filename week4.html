Digital Learning Resources and Multimedia

Using text, images, audio, video, and interactive media

Week Overview

Digital learning resources present information in different formats. Good multimedia design makes ideas easier to understand by combining suitable words, visuals, sound, and interaction without overloading learners.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:

  • Identify common types of digital learning resources.
  • Explain how multimedia can support understanding and memory.
  • Apply basic principles for clear and accessible learning materials.
  • Evaluate the quality and suitability of an online resource.

Topics and Study Notes

Types of resources

Examples include e books, presentations, educational websites, podcasts, animations, simulations, infographics, and interactive worksheets.

Multimedia learning

Words and visuals can work together to explain a concept. Each media element should have a clear learning purpose.

Design principles

Keep layouts clear, use readable text, group related information, remove unnecessary decoration, and divide complex explanations into manageable parts.

Accessibility and quality

Use captions, alternative text, clear contrast, reliable sources, and age appropriate language. Check accuracy, relevance, and copyright before sharing resources.

Related Study Material

  1. Find one educational video and one infographic on the same topic.
  2. Evaluate a digital resource for accuracy, relevance, readability, and accessibility.
  3. Prepare a short multimedia explanation of a concept.

Suggested video resource: Watch related educational videos on YouTube

Class Activity

Create a one slide learning resource with a title, a short explanation, one relevant visual, and a source note. Ask a classmate to review its clarity.
