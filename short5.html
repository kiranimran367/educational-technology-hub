10 Short Questions with Answers

Each question is followed by a clear answer for revision and exam preparation.

  1. What is a Learning Management System?

    Answer: An LMS is a digital platform used to organise courses, share learning materials, communicate, collect assignments, conduct quizzes, and monitor progress.

  2. Name two examples of an LMS.

    Answer: Moodle and Google Classroom are examples of learning management platforms.

  3. Write four common functions of an LMS.

    Answer: An LMS may provide course pages, announcements, file sharing, assignment submission, quizzes, discussion forums, and gradebooks.

  4. What is synchronous learning?

    Answer: Synchronous learning takes place when teachers and learners participate at the same time, such as in a live online class.

  5. What is asynchronous learning?

    Answer: Asynchronous learning allows students to use recorded lessons, readings, and tasks at different times.

  6. Differentiate synchronous and asynchronous learning.

    Answer: Synchronous learning happens at a scheduled shared time. Asynchronous learning does not require everyone to be online together.

  7. What is blended learning?

    Answer: Blended learning combines face to face teaching with planned online learning activities.

  8. Give one example of blended learning.

    Answer: Students discuss a topic in class, watch a related online video at home, and submit a reflection through an LMS.

  9. Mention two advantages of online learning.

    Answer: It provides flexible access to materials and allows students to study from different locations or review recorded lessons.

  10. How can teachers support online learners?

    Answer: Teachers can give clear instructions, organise materials, provide timely feedback, communicate regularly, and explain where students can get help.
