Exploring AI, augmented reality, virtual reality, and simulations

Week Overview

Emerging technologies can create new ways to explain ideas, practise skills, and personalise learning. Their value depends on educational purpose, evidence, teacher guidance, access, safety, and responsible use.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:

  • Describe educational uses of artificial intelligence.
  • Differentiate augmented reality and virtual reality.
  • Explain how simulations can support practice and exploration.
  • Identify risks and responsible practices when using emerging technologies.

Topics and Study Notes

Artificial intelligence

AI tools can support idea generation, language practice, tutoring, feedback, and content adaptation. Outputs must be checked for accuracy and bias.

Augmented reality

AR adds digital information or objects to a view of the real world, often through a phone or tablet.

Virtual reality

VR creates an immersive digital environment that learners can explore using a headset or other equipment.

Simulations and virtual labs

Simulations let learners explore systems and practise decisions in a controlled environment.

Responsible use

Consider privacy, consent, bias, accessibility, cost, screen time, academic honesty, and the need for human judgement.

Related Study Material

  1. Find an example of an AI tool used for learning and check its limitations.
  2. Compare AR and VR using one educational example for each.
  3. Read a case study about a virtual lab or educational simulation.

Suggested video resource: Watch related educational videos on YouTube

Class Activity

Choose one emerging technology and propose a lesson activity. Explain the learning benefit, possible risk, and how a teacher would manage it.
