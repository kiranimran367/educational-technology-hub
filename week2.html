Learning Theories and Technology Integration

Connecting educational technology with how people learn

Week Overview

Technology should be selected according to learning goals and learners' needs. Learning theories help teachers understand how students gain knowledge, practise skills, interact with others, and build understanding.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:

  • Explain behaviourism, cognitivism, and constructivism.
  • Describe social learning and connectivism in digital settings.
  • Match digital tools with learning goals and learning theories.
  • Explain why pedagogy should guide technology selection.

Topics and Study Notes

Behaviourism

Learning is shaped by practice, feedback, and reinforcement. Digital quizzes, flashcards, and drill activities can provide immediate feedback.

Cognitivism

Learning involves attention, memory, and information processing. Concept maps, structured slides, and short multimedia explanations can help organise information.

Constructivism

Learners build understanding through exploration and problem solving. Simulations, projects, and inquiry activities support active learning.

Social learning and connectivism

People learn by observing others and by forming connections with people and information. Discussion boards, peer review, and collaborative documents can support these approaches.

Choosing a suitable tool

Start with the learning outcome, learner needs, and teaching method. Then choose a tool that supports the task rather than using technology for its own sake.

Related Study Material

  1. Review the main ideas of behaviourism, cognitivism, and constructivism.
  2. Create a comparison chart of theories, teacher role, learner role, and suitable digital tools.
  3. Read a short case study about collaborative online learning.

Suggested video resource: Watch related educational videos on YouTube

Class Activity

Select one topic from a school subject. Design a short learning task using any one learning theory and explain how the digital tool supports it.
