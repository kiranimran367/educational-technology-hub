10 Short Questions with Answers

Each question is followed by a clear answer for revision and exam preparation.

  1. What is digital assessment?

    Answer: Digital assessment uses electronic tools to collect and evaluate evidence of student learning.

  2. What is formative assessment?

    Answer: Formative assessment takes place during learning to check progress and help teachers improve instruction.

  3. What is summative assessment?

    Answer: Summative assessment measures achievement at the end of a unit, course, or learning period.

  4. Give two examples of formative digital assessment.

    Answer: Online polls and short quizzes are examples. Exit tickets and discussion prompts can also be used.

  5. Give two examples of summative assessment.

    Answer: Final online tests and digital projects are examples. Presentations and completed e portfolios may also be used.

  6. What is feedback?

    Answer: Feedback is information given to learners about their performance, including what they did well and how they can improve.

  7. What makes feedback effective?

    Answer: Effective feedback is clear, specific, respectful, timely, and connected to the learning objectives.

  8. What are learning analytics?

    Answer: Learning analytics involves collecting and examining information about learning activities, such as quiz scores and assignment completion.

  9. What is validity in assessment?

    Answer: Validity means that an assessment measures the knowledge or skill it is intended to measure.

  10. Why is privacy important in digital assessment?

    Answer: Digital assessments may contain names, grades, and personal work. This information should be protected and shared only with authorised people.
