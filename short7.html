10 Short Questions with Answers

Each question is followed by a clear answer for revision and exam preparation.

  1. What are emerging technologies in education?

    Answer: They are new or rapidly developing tools that may change teaching and learning, such as AI, AR, VR, simulations, and adaptive learning systems.

  2. What is artificial intelligence?

    Answer: AI is technology that performs tasks such as recognising patterns, generating text, translating language, or responding to questions.

  3. Give two educational uses of AI.

    Answer: AI can support language practice and personalised tutoring. It can also help generate ideas and provide feedback.

  4. What is augmented reality?

    Answer: Augmented reality adds digital information or objects to a view of the real world, often through a phone or tablet.

  5. What is virtual reality?

    Answer: Virtual reality creates an immersive computer generated environment that learners can explore, often using a headset.

  6. Differentiate AR and VR.

    Answer: AR adds digital objects to the real world, while VR places the learner inside a fully digital environment.

  7. What is a simulation?

    Answer: A simulation is a digital model of a real or imagined process that allows learners to explore and practise in a controlled setting.

  8. How can virtual laboratories help students?

    Answer: They allow students to practise experiments and explore scientific processes when physical equipment, time, or safety is limited.

  9. Mention two risks of using AI in education.

    Answer: AI may produce inaccurate or biased information, and it may create privacy or academic honesty concerns.

  10. Why is teacher guidance important when using emerging technologies?

    Answer: Teachers help learners use tools safely, check information, understand limitations, and connect activities with learning objectives.
